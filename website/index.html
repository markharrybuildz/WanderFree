What is WanderFreely?

A simple app for people who use rewards credit cards. It tracks the cards you hold, your progress toward signup bonuses, the recurring benefits and credits on each card, and your points across every program — so you actually use the value you're paying for.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. WanderFreely never asks for full card numbers, security codes, or bank logins, and it does not connect to your financial accounts. Your data is stored securely and protected by per-user access rules so only you can see it. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I join the beta?

Email wanderfree.app@gmail.com and we'll send you an invite. WanderFreely is free during the beta.

Which cards are supported?

We're expanding the catalog continuously. If a card you hold isn't there yet, let us know and we'll add it.

How do I delete my account?

Any time, right from the app: go to Account → Delete account, and your data is permanently removed. You can also email us to request deletion.
